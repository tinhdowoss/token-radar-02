How a value is built

  1. Fit log–log on live comps: log10(mcap) against log10(TVL).
  2. Apply the category offset, shrunk toward the global median so thin categories do not swing the value.
  3. Bootstrap 200 refits to get the P10–P90 range. Growth and age are not multiplied into the price — they made out-of-sample error worse.
